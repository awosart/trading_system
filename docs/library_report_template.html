Equity-curve correlation

For checking intent, not for selection: if strategies built around different ideas produce correlated out-of-sample curves, they are exposed to the market the same way, whatever their entry logic claims. This is computed over full daily returns, flat (no-position) days included — see the module docstring on why that, and not restricting to days both strategies were active, is the quantity that actually answers “is this twenty strategies or one bet with the risk turned up.” A cell reads “—” when the two out-of-sample windows share fewer than {{ min_shared_days }} overlapping trading days. The count under each figure is how many of those shared days both strategies actually held a position — look at it before trusting the coefficient above it; a correlation resting on a handful of co-active days is not the same claim as one resting on hundreds.
